KnowledgeForge

A portable content pipeline that turns AI-assisted technical problem solving into durable knowledge while preserving privacy, provenance, and human authority.

The problem

Years of useful troubleshooting and design work can disappear inside conversations. Extracting it carelessly risks publishing secrets, losing source context, or presenting unverified guidance as fact.

The model

Sources move through inbox, immutable raw archive, privacy review, classification, structured knowledge, synthesis, technical verification, and human-controlled publication. Stable identifiers, exact-byte checksums, conflict handling, and explicit state transitions make each asset traceable.

Verified state

Milestone 1 and 2 exit assessments passed, a bounded real-export pilot completed, and the pipeline advanced to classification and redaction evaluation. Bulk ingestion and real-source processing remain separately disabled until explicitly approved.
