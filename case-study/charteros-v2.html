Platform architecture · 2026

CharterOS V2

A clean second-generation operations platform planned from verified capabilities—not from assumptions about production state, data rights, or provider access.

The architecture

A single-entity modular monolith with PostgreSQL as the transactional source of truth, durable workers, object storage, and outbox/inbox patterns for external side effects. Pricing remains deterministic; AI may assist, but cannot silently approve money movement, dispatch, publishing, or customer communication.

The delivery boundary

M0 established evidence capture, architecture decisions, synthetic golden journeys, domain contracts, threat modeling, source remediation, a Docker Compose model, and Proxmox staging. The next slice is restricted to synthetic, no-network lead-to-approved-quote behavior.

What is not claimed

No production code migration, customer-data transfer, payment action, external message, provider cutover, or production validation is represented as complete.
