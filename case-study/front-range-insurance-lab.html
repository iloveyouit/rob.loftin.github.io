Front Range Insurance Lab

A reusable bilingual reference experience for insurance agencies, built with content boundaries and deployment portability in mind.

The product

The concept combines English and Spanish education, adaptive intake, location-specific journeys, comparison priorities, secure handoff, and privacy-aware saved plans without representing a fictional agency as a live business.

The delivery model

Standalone Node output, Docker/VPS packaging, AMD64 CI coverage, and documented deployment make the experience portable beyond a single hosted platform.

Verified state

The container path and CI coverage were validated, privacy boundaries and bilingual behavior were checked, and the owner-only deployment remained private and noindex. It is a governed concept and starter—not a public carrier, quote engine, or operating agency.
