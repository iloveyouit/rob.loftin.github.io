Patch Compliance & Recovery Automation

A safer monthly patch workflow: prove reachability, establish recovery, validate the actual security baseline, and leave reviewable evidence.

The problem

A successful Windows Update task does not prove that a server reached the required security baseline. Maintenance also needs a confirmed recovery path before installation begins.

The workflow

  • Validate WinRM plus RPC, WMI, or CIM connectivity and separate unreachable systems from non-compliant systems.
  • Check Windows Server 2016, 2019, 2022, and 2025 against the month’s required KB and build.
  • Produce console, CSV, and HTML evidence for change and compliance review.
  • Prepare managed OS and data-disk snapshots with WhatIf, duplicate detection, per-disk status, and fail-safe readiness gates.

Verified state

The April 2026 compliance workflow and its reporting were developed and validated. The companion snapshot workflow was prepared for a 12-VM Azure scope. Target-subscription execution, retention, and application-consistency controls remain environment-specific validation steps.
