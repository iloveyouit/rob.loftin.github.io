Infrastructure Evidence Toolkit

Reusable investigation patterns that narrow the fault domain before anyone makes a disruptive change.

The problem

Monitoring signals, archived events, remote-management failures, and incomplete inventories often create pressure to act before the underlying condition is understood.

The toolkit

  • WinRM discovery and GPO/configuration validation.
  • WSUS exposure and maintenance checks.
  • Archived Active Directory account-change investigation.
  • Domain controller and hypervisor discovery.
  • Detailed Windows reboot-event capture and structured output.

Operating principle

Each workflow records prerequisites, scope, failure states, and evidence. In one investigation, endpoint validation showed that WinRM itself was healthy despite a monitoring property reporting otherwise—avoiding an unnecessary service change and redirecting troubleshooting to the correct layer.
